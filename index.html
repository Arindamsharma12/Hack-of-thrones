About The Realm

Welcome to Hack Of Thrones - The Game of Codes, where the Seven Kingdoms of Technology unite for an epic battle of innovation. In this 24-hour hackathon, you will forge alliances, build powerful solutions, and claim the Iron Throne of Technology.

Just as in Game of Thrones, success requires strategy, skill, and determination. Teams of up to four members will compete across various houses (tracks) including Web Development, Mobile Applications, Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, and Internet of Things.

Winter is Coming... and so is your chance to showcase your technological prowess and creative problem-solving abilities. Join us for a night of coding, collaboration, and conquest!

Ravens' Knowledge

Who can participate in Hack Of Thrones?
Any student, professional, or tech enthusiast can participate. Teams can have up to 4 members. Participants must be at least 18 years old.
What should I bring to the hackathon?
Bring your laptop, charger, any hardware you need for your project, a valid ID, and your creativity. We'll provide food, drinks, and a place to work.
Is there a registration fee?
No, participation is free of charge thanks to our generous sponsors. However, registration is required and spots are limited, so register early!
Will there be food and accommodations?
Yes, meals, snacks, and beverages will be provided throughout the 24 hours. We do not provide accommodations, but we have partnered with nearby hotels for discounted rates.
Can I start working on my project before the hackathon?
No, all projects must be started and completed during the 24-hour hackathon period. Pre-existing projects are not allowed.
What kind of projects can I build?
You can build any software or hardware project that aligns with our tracks: Web Development, Mobile Applications, Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, or Internet of Things.
